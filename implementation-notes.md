### How it works
- DriveAssist: `routes` empty = the old code path, untouched line for line (only `DistanceNearest`, `CourseLength` and the number keys were reshaped to branch, with the same results). With routes: each `CourseRoute` gets 3D cumulative distance (closed: plus the step from the last point to the first), and per point a flat right (central differences) and a heading. The lane line is the one the ring paves and the sweep checks (`path + right * lane`, straight between points), not the single course's per-segment right; the car's heading is eased between point headings, so bends of up to 7.6 degrees per point no longer snap. Nearest-point search projects onto lane-line segments with the old height weight (dx² + dz² + 4 dy²).
- Auto-drive (T) starts on the route whose lane line is nearest (the active one keeps a tie), eases in from up to 4 m off the lane, wraps at the end of a closed route, and moves over to `preferredRoute` only where both centre lines are the same road here and 70 m on (so the lane change, eased over 60 m, is done before a fork; later it waits for the next shared road). F toggles the preferred route; the guide (G) shows `지금 경로: <active>    F 갈림길 선택: <preferred>`.
- Active route (manual driving, checked every 0.2 s of game time): left when the car is more than 2.5 m from its lane line and another route's lane line is within 1.0 m or 3 m nearer, and only where the routes part (50 m on along the active route the other's lane line is more than 3 m away). R and leaving the drive bounds return to the nearest lane point of all routes (active, then preferred, keep a tie). Jumps carry their route (`jumpRoutes`).
- Bridge courserun: `route=<i>` makes route i preferred, starts on it, shows the guide and judges progress by unwrapped distance on it; `laps=<n>`; `lane=<m>` offsets the steering target and the lane-deviation measure; `shotsEvery=<m>` stops the bot, waits 3 frames (VehicleCamera has moved the camera in LateUpdate) and renders `drive-<n>-p<progress>.png`; the first time above y 29 it also saves `game-view-top.png` (Game view with the guide). `startOn=<k>` starts on route k's lane while steering route i.
